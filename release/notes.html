MindExtract 3.1

  • Save the video from the page you're on. Open the notch over Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Brave or Vivaldi, and a YouTube or Vimeo video is ready to save or transcribe. No copying needed.
  • Find Video on other pages, like webinars and conference talks. MindExtract looks for the video only when you click, and reads nothing but the page's address and title.
  • Webinar recordings save in full, even when their sound is encrypted.
  • Pasting a link to a page MindExtract doesn't know now finds the video on it.
