MindExtract 3.0
Download and transcribe video from anywhere, right from the notch.
© 2026 Mindact Solutions AB


──────────────────────────────────────────────
  INSTALL
──────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Drag MindExtract.app to the Applications folder.
  2. Open MindExtract from Applications.
  3. The notch introduces itself and downloads the
     speech model (630 MB, once).


──────────────────────────────────────────────
  HOW IT WORKS
──────────────────────────────────────────────

  Open the notch
    Rest the pointer on the notch, click it, or
    press Control Option Space from any app.

  Paste a link or drag a file
    YouTube and over a thousand other sites.
    Choose Transcribe, or Save Video.

  Or just open it over your browser
    The video on the page you're on is ready
    to save or transcribe. On other pages,
    click Find Video.

  Paste the text anywhere
    The finished transcript is copied for you.
    Press Command V in Claude, ChatGPT or any app.


──────────────────────────────────────────────
  SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
──────────────────────────────────────────────

  • macOS 26 or later
  • Apple Silicon or Intel Mac
  • Internet connection for fetching videos and,
    once, the speech model


──────────────────────────────────────────────
  PRIVACY
──────────────────────────────────────────────

  Transcription runs on your Mac. Nothing is
  uploaded. Only the video itself is fetched
  from the site. A copied link from a video
  site is looked up as the notch opens, so
  its sizes are ready. Over a browser, only
  the page's address and title are read;
  other pages are fetched only when you click
  Find Video.
