What's New in 2.6.0

MindExtract becomes the app you open every day — not just when you have a meeting. Your meetings now remember each other.

☀️ Today

  • A new home screen: today's meetings, every open commitment across all your meetings, and your latest recaps — in one glance.

✅ Open commitments that follow you

  • Action items from every meeting are gathered into one checkable list, so nothing you promised quietly slips. Check them off as you go.

🤝 People & Topics

  • Every person you've met is threaded automatically — all your meetings with them and what's still open between you. Recurring meetings cluster into project threads. No tagging, no data entry.

✨ Never walk in cold

  • Before a scheduled meeting, a Prep card shows what you discussed last time with these people and what's still open with them.

Everything is 100% on-device — it reads only what MindExtract already saved on your Mac.
