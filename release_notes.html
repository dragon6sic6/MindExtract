What's New in 2.5.1

Meeting detection that just works — and finds you wherever you are.

🎯 Detects Teams, Meet & more — reliably

  • Fixed detection for new Microsoft Teams and browser calls (Google Meet, Zoom/Teams web). MindExtract now identifies the app actually capturing your microphone — including helper processes — so it recognizes the call regardless of how the app is built.

🔔 A nudge wherever you are

  • When you're in a call and MindExtract is in the background, it sends a gentle notification — tap Record to capture it. No need to have the app open. (Turn off in Settings → Recording.)

🔗 Paste a meeting link

  • Have a Teams/Zoom/Meet link but haven't joined yet? Paste it on the Record screen for one-tap recording.
