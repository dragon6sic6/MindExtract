What's New in 2.1.4

A big update — a deep pass over the whole app: transcripts, history, settings, downloads, and the AI chat.

Chat with your transcript

  • Chat from anywhere — a dedicated, roomy Chat view you open from any tab. Ask questions and the AI answers from the transcript; tap a tab to jump back to reading.
  • Switch AI models in the chat — each model shows whether it's ready to use, and if one fails (e.g. a missing key) you get a one-click "Try again" with a different model — no retyping.
  • Suggested questions to get started, copy any answer, and clear the conversation.
  • Summaries & chats are saved with the transcript and restored when you reopen it.

Transcription

  • Cancel keeps your work — stopping a transcription now saves what was done so far instead of discarding it.
  • Better search — match count and next/previous navigation that jumps through the transcript.
  • Rename speakers — now clearly labelled and easy to find.
  • Set a default language once in Settings instead of choosing it every time.
  • Play/pause with the Space bar, click a segment to play from there, and the elapsed time shows while transcribing.

Downloads & History

  • Transcribe straight from History — past downloads now remember where the file was saved, so you can transcribe, reveal in Finder, or see when a file has been deleted.
  • Queue control — pause/resume, retry failed downloads, and download several qualities or a whole playlist as audio.
  • Paste several links at once to queue them all.
  • Filter and sort your download history; audio downloads now appear in History too.

Settings

  • Check for Updates right from Settings, test your OpenAI/Anthropic key, reveal your downloads or app-data folder, and reset settings to defaults.

Polish & fixes

  • Buttons across the app are now fully clickable (not just the icon), with consistent styling and clearer status messages.
  • The interface adapts cleanly at any window size — no more text breaking mid-word.
  • Many real bug fixes along the way, including downloads, the queue, audio history, and the transcript view.
