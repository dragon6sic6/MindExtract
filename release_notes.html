What's New in 2.8.0

The biggest update yet — MindExtract becomes a calm daily command center, captures meetings smarter, and lets your AI act on everything you've heard.

☀️ Today is now a command center

  • Focus — the few things that matter right now (next meeting + most pressing commitments), each with its action.
  • Join & Record in one tap for calendar meetings with a link; snooze, reschedule, and nudge people who owe you, right from a commitment.
  • Calm by design: gentle amber instead of red alarms, one primary action per row, quick-actions menu, Focus mode.

📅 Smarter, cleaner meeting detection

  • A floating "Meeting detected — Record?" prompt you can't miss, plus an orange menu-bar indicator and a notification.
  • Today shows only your meetings — a colleague's events on a shared calendar are filtered out, and duplicate copies of the same meeting are merged.

🗣️ Language that just works

  • Auto-detects the spoken language and switches to KB-Whisper for Swedish automatically.
  • Wrong language? Re-transcribe on the same audio in one click.
  • The meeting title now travels into the transcript and the saved file name.

✨ Recipes — your after-meeting ritual, automated

  • Run a fixed set of steps — Brief → format (SOAP/Minutes/Legal) → Reminders → recap → export → hand to your AI — on demand or automatically after every meeting. On-device, no logins.

🔌 World-class MCP

  • Your AI (Claude/ChatGPT) can now read your meetings, briefs, action items and people, and file results into your own tools — fully on-device, nothing uploaded.

🧹 A clearer transcript screen

  • One canonical AI Brief you can reshape with "Summarize as…" — no more confusing duplicate summaries.

Everything stays on your Mac, as always.
