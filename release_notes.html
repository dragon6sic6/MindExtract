What's New in 2.7.0

A big upgrade to Today — the commitments are now real, structured and trustworthy, so it's the screen you actually start your day with.

✅ Commitments that make sense

  • Action items are now read structurally from each meeting's Brief — the task, who owns it, and the due date — instead of scraping raw text. No more "Owner: No owner mentioned" noise or duplicates.
  • Split into Yours vs Waiting on others, so follow-through is obvious.
  • Grouped by urgency with due-date chips (Overdue · Due today · This week). One click to push any item to Reminders with its real due date.
  • Right-click to edit a commitment or remove one that isn't a real task.

🌅 Plan your day at a glance

  • A one-line summary at the top: meetings left, what's overdue, what's due today, and what's next.

🏷️ Meetings name themselves

  • Recordings without a calendar title are now auto-named from their Brief, so People and Topics group cleanly.

⚙️ New: Settings → Today

  • Tell MindExtract who you are (name + email) to power "yours vs theirs", choose how many days ahead to show, and toggle the daily summary.

All on-device, as always.
