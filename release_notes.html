What's New in 2.5.0

Meetings, made effortless. Capture without thinking, mark what matters, and walk away with a brief you can act on and share in one tap — all still fully on your Mac.

✨ The Meeting Brief

  • When a meeting finishes, MindExtract writes one clear brief automatically: a TL;DR, the decisions, and the action items — the first thing you see, ready to use.

🚩 Mark the moments that matter

  • Press ⌘M (or tap Mark moment) any time during a recording. Your bookmarks appear in the brief as clickable key moments that jump straight to that point in the audio.

📧 One-tap recap to attendees

  • Send a follow-up email with the recap — pre-addressed to the meeting's attendees and pre-filled with the brief. Closes the loop in seconds.

🎙️ Thoughtless capture

  • Turn on Start recording automatically and MindExtract begins the moment a meeting or call is detected — no need to remember to hit record. Stop any time with one tap.
