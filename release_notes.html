What's New in 2.4.0

Pro tools for people who work with transcripts every day — edit, brand, batch, and lock — all still fully on your Mac.

✏️ Edit your transcripts

  • Fix a mis-heard word, name or term right in the transcript. Tap Edit transcript in the Timeline, tap any line, and your change is saved with the file.

🔒 Lock the app (Touch ID / password)

  • Turn on Require Touch ID or password to open so client, patient or case material isn't visible to anyone at an unlocked Mac. (For full at-rest disk encryption, use macOS FileVault.)

🧾 Branded PDF deliverables

  • Add your business name and logo in Settings and export transcripts and notes as a clean, letterheaded PDF — ready to hand to a client. Redacted PDFs carry a clear privacy notice.

📁 Batch / folder transcription

  • Drop a folder (or several files) and hit Transcribe all — MindExtract works through them one by one and saves each to Transcripts. Perfect for researchers and anyone with a backlog.

Under the hood

  • First automated test suite + structured logging for a more stable app, and a faster, more discoverable Search & Ask.
