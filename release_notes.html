What's New in MindExtract 2.0

MindExtract has been redesigned from the ground up — new look, new engine, new flow.

✨ A whole new app

  • All-new dark design with Liquid Glass — clean, sharp, and native to modern macOS
  • One Media surface — paste any link or drop a file. The app figures out whether it's a video, a playlist, or a local file. No more choosing modes
  • New Transcripts section — all your transcripts in one searchable place, organized by date
  • Transcription happens right in the app — no more separate window

🎙️ Transcription, supercharged

  • New Apple speech engine (macOS 26) — dramatically faster transcription that starts instantly, with nothing to download. WhisperKit remains available for older Macs
  • Rename speakers — click "Speaker 1" and give them real names; names follow into exports
  • Follow-along playback — the transcript highlights and scrolls in sync while you listen
  • Clear choices up front — pick engine, language, and speaker labels right before you start

⬇️ Better downloads

  • Clean quality picker — choose 4K/1080p/720p instead of scrolling 100+ technical formats. Downloads always play in QuickTime
  • Progress on the row — the quality you picked fills up as it downloads
  • Playlists made easy — paste a playlist link, select the videos you want, download them all

💬 Quality of life

  • Human error messages — plain language instead of technical dumps, with a one-click "Sign in to YouTube" when that's the fix
  • Faster loading — links load quicker, and the downloader warms up at launch
  • Simpler Settings — reorganized with explanations and tooltips; the setup guide is gone because there's nothing to set up anymore

⚙️ Requirements

  • MindExtract now requires macOS 14 or later. The Apple speech engine requires macOS 26; on older systems WhisperKit is used automatically
