What's New in 2.5.2

MindExtract now nudges you the moment a scheduled meeting starts — even when it's in the background.

📅 Records find you on time

  • When a meeting on your calendar begins, MindExtract sends a notification — tap Record to capture it with the title and attendees already filled in. Combined with live-call detection, you'll never miss the start of a meeting again.
  • Detection now runs app-wide (no need to have the Record screen open). Turn nudges off any time in Settings → Recording.
