What's New in 2.3.0

The biggest update yet: a complete meeting suite, a private searchable memory of everything you've recorded, and a redesigned Settings — all still fully on your Mac.

🎙️ The world's-best meeting recorder

  • Calendar detection — MindExtract sees the meeting you're in and offers to record it with one tap, attendees pre-filled. Add your Google or Microsoft account in System Settings and those meetings show up too.
  • Live-call detection — even without a calendar event, it notices when you're in a Zoom, Teams, Webex or browser call and offers to record.
  • “You vs. them” speaker labelling — your voice and the remote participants are told apart automatically.
  • Floating live captions that stay on top of Zoom/Teams while you record.
  • Talk-time insights — see who spoke how much, turns taken, questions asked, and the longest monologue.
  • Automatic notes — Meeting Minutes and Action Items are generated the moment a recording finishes transcribing.

🧠 Your private second brain

  • Search inside every transcript at once — find any word across everything you've ever recorded.
  • Ask across everything — “What did we decide about X?” answered from all your transcripts, with sources cited.
  • ⌘K command palette — search and ask from anywhere in the app.

🎯 Accuracy, output & privacy

  • Custom vocabulary — add names and domain terms so they're spelled correctly every time.
  • PDF export and one-tap PII redaction — mask names, emails and phone numbers before sharing.
  • The full KB-Whisper range for Swedish (Base, Small, Medium, Large), with a clearer model list.
  • Send action items to Reminders in one click.

🔌 Connect to Claude & ChatGPT (MCP)

  • Expose your transcripts to Claude Desktop (or any MCP client) as a local tool — ask your favourite AI to search and pull from your recordings. Runs on your Mac; nothing is uploaded by MindExtract.

⚙️ Redesigned Settings & organization

  • A clean category layout, full calendar management, a microphone picker, download quality, auto-transcribe on download, and transcription-complete notifications.
  • Type markers (Meeting / Download / File) and favorites to find things fast, plus a quick first-run welcome.
