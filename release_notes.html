MindExtract 3.0

MindExtract now lives in your Mac's notch. Paste a link, get the video or the text. That's the whole app, and it's faster than ever.

Lives in the notch

  • Rest the pointer on the notch, click it, or press ⌃⌥Space from any app. You can pick your own shortcut.
  • No Dock icon and no windows in your way. It starts with your Mac, so it's always there.
  • On a Mac without a notch, it sits at the top center of the screen.

Link in, text out

  • Copied a YouTube link? The notch already sees it. One click and it transcribes.
  • Works with YouTube and over a thousand other sites. Save the video too if you want it.
  • Drag a video or a recording from Finder onto the notch to transcribe a file.

Ready to paste into Claude or ChatGPT

  • When the transcript is done it's already on your clipboard. Just press ⌘V.
  • The title, link and channel come along at the top, so your AI knows what it's reading.
  • Reads like an article by default. Turn on timestamps in Settings if you want them.
  • When several people speak, each turn is marked with who said it.

Swedish at its best

  • Pianissimo, the best Swedish speech model, downloads once on Swedish Macs and is used automatically for Swedish audio.

Recording meetings is gone

  • MindExtract 3.0 does one thing well: videos and recordings in, text out. Meeting recording, dictation and summaries have been removed.
  • Your old recordings and transcripts stay on your Mac, untouched. The short introduction shows you where they are.

Also new

  • Cancel really stops the job, and nothing half finished is saved.
  • Clearer messages when a download fails, with a way forward.
  • Age restricted or members only videos: sign in by borrowing the sign in from your browser. YouTube has ended the old sign in method.
  • The latest download engine, for sites that changed recently.
  • Settings rebuilt from scratch: fewer choices, each one explained.

Everything still happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. Requires macOS 26.
